# Dictionary data notice

`nan.tsv`, `hak.tsv` and `cmn.tsv` are generated by `tools/build-dict.mjs` from Ministry of Education (Taiwan) dictionaries:

| File | Source | Machine-readable form used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `nan.tsv` | 教育部《臺灣閩南語常用詞辭典》 <https://sutian.moe.edu.tw/> | <https://github.com/g0v/moedict-data-twblg> |
| `hak.tsv` | 教育部《臺灣客家語常用詞辭典》 <https://hakkadict.moe.edu.tw/> | <https://github.com/g0v/moedict-data-hakka> |
| `cmn.tsv` | 教育部《重編國語辭典修訂本》 <https://dict.revised.moe.edu.tw/> | <https://github.com/g0v/moedict-data> |

The dictionary text is © 中華民國教育部 (Ministry of Education, R.O.C.), released under
[CC BY-ND 3.0 Taiwan](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/nd/3.0/tw/deed.zh_TW) (姓名標示-禁止改作).
Attribution: 教育部. Per the MOE's interpretation, the no-derivatives clause covers the dictionary text itself and
does not restrict format conversion or reuse. The conversion here only changes the format (headwords and readings are
copied unchanged apart from Unicode NFC normalisation), keeps entries of up to 6 characters (Mandarin: 4), and drops
definitions, examples and entries using private-use characters. The g0v conversions are released by their editors under CC0.

Hakka readings are the MOE's Sixian (四縣) forms; the Hailu, Dapu, Raoping, Zhao'an and South Sixian spellings are
searchable but not displayed.

Rebuild: download the three JSON files, then `node tools/build-dict.mjs <dir>`.

## Indigenous languages (`ind/*.tsv`)

Generated by `tools/build-formosan.mjs` from the 學習詞表 (learning vocabulary) of 族語E樂園 (ePark / Klokah,
<https://web.klokah.tw/>, 原住民族語言研究發展基金會 ILRDF), as published in the FormosanBank corpus
(<https://github.com/FormosanBank/FormosanBank>, `Corpora/ePark`). The data is licensed
[CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/): attribution required, **non-commercial use
only**, adaptations must be shared under the same licence. Please also cite:

- Indigenous Languages Research and Development Foundation. (2020). 族語E樂園. <https://web.klokah.tw/>
- Mohamed, W., Le Ferrand, E., Sung, L.-M., Prud'hommeaux, E., & Hartshorne, J. K. (2024). FormosanBank.

One file per language (16 languages: Amis, Atayal, Bunun, Paiwan, Rukai, Puyuma, Tsou, Saisiyat, Yami, Thao, Kavalan,
Truku, Sakizaya, Kanakanavu, Saaroa, Seediq). Dialects of a language are merged. Each row is the standard orthography
of a word with its Chinese gloss; notes, sentences and rows that are not plain words are dropped.

## English (`eng.tsv`)

Generated by `tools/build-english.mjs`: the 20,000 most frequent words of the FrequencyWords list
(<https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords>, built from OpenSubtitles, CC BY-SA 4.0), kept only if they are in
`words_alpha.txt` from <https://github.com/dwyl/english-words> (Unlicense), with a short profanity blocklist removed.
